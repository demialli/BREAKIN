*OOP Map Game Idea*

Synopsis: You're lost in your friend's new house at a sleepover and you need to find your way back to your room.
Objective: Get back to your bedroom (R3) before 3am.

*Rooms*
R0: kitchen
- Start

R1: hallway
- Stool here
- Needed to open bathroom door

R2: living room (flashlight here)
- Watch here
- Cat NPC here
- Guides you to room five

R3: bedroom (needs key to unlock)
- End
- Game finish upon going to sleep

R4: bathroom (needs stool to unlock)
- Guiding message to R2 if visited first
- Optional to visit

R5: study (bedroom key here)
- Bedroom key is in a desk here


*Items*
ITEM: watch
- Used to check time
ITEM_SLOT_0: stool
- Used to open R4
ITEM_SLOT_1: key
- Used to open R3
NPC: talking cat
- Guides you to R5


*Mechanisms*
- Message upon trying to open a locked door
- Command to check time on watch so player can see how long they have
