*OOP Map Game Idea*

Synopsis: You're a burglar trying to get to a locked safe in a house before the end of the night.
Objective: Unlock the safe's room (R3) before everyone wakes up at 6am.

*Rooms*
R0: kitchen
- Start

R1: living room
- Key here
- Needed to open study

R2: hallway (watch here)
- Pocket watch here
- Burglar NPC here
- Guides you to room five

R3: safe (needs diary to unlock)
- End
- Game finish upon getting to safe

R4: study (needs key to unlock)
- Guiding message to R2 if visited first
- Optional to visit

R5: bedroom (diary here)
- Diary is in a desk here
- Code to unlock safe in diary


*Items*
ITEM: watch
- Used to check time
ITEM_SLOT_0: key
- Used to open R4
ITEM_SLOT_1: diary
- Used to open R3
NPC: burglar
- Guides you to R5


*Mechanisms*
- Message upon trying to open a locked door
- Command to check time on pocket watch so player can see how long they have
